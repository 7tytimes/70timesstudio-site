Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

70times Studio LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, how it may be stored or retained, and how it is protected when you use our website, products, games, demos, services, and related applications, including DUP - Multiplayer.

Information We Collect

We may collect or process the following information:

  • Email addresses voluntarily provided for support requests.
  • Technical information such as crash logs, error reports, device information, and diagnostic data.
  • Epic Games Account ID, authentication status, and platform-related information provided through Epic Games services.
  • Multiplayer session, matchmaking, lobby, friends, invite, and network connection information required for online gameplay.
  • Basic gameplay information required to operate online features, prevent abuse, fix errors, and maintain service stability.
  • Locally stored game settings, preferences, or save data stored on the player's device.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate players through Epic Online Services (EOS).
  • Enable multiplayer gameplay, matchmaking, sessions, lobbies, and friend invitations.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our products, demos, and services.
  • Fix bugs, crashes, and technical issues.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and protect online services.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Data Storage and Retention

70times Studio LLC does not sell personal information.

Information provided directly to us, such as support emails, may be retained only for as long as necessary to respond to the request, provide support, resolve technical issues, protect our rights, or comply with legal obligations.

Data processed through Epic Games Store or Epic Online Services (EOS), including authentication, account, multiplayer session, lobby, matchmaking, friends, and invite-related data, may be stored or retained by Epic Games according to Epic Games' own privacy policy and data retention practices.

Locally stored game settings, preferences, or save data may remain on the player's device until the player deletes the data, uninstalls the game, or resets the related local files.

Third-Party Services

Our products may integrate with third-party services including:

  • Epic Games Store
  • Epic Online Services (EOS)
  • GitHub
  • Cloudflare
  • Google Services

These services may collect, process, store, or retain information according to their own privacy policies.

Epic Games Privacy Policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacy-policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when required to provide services, operate online features, comply with legal obligations, investigate abuse, protect our rights, or maintain security.

Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our products and services are not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
